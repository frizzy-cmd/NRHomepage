Forum Guidelines & Privacy

Community Guidelines & Privacy Policy

Welcome to the community forum! To ensure a safe, welcoming, and friendly space for modders, coders, and other users, please review the rules and privacy disclosures below.

Guidelines

Rule 1: Respect
Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, discrimination, or abusive behavior will result in an immediate mute or suspension. This includes targeting users based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, or any other personal characteristic.
Rule 2: Excessive posting
Do not spam posts, comments, repetitive text, or malicious links. Attempting to bypass rate limits or any attempts to do malicious acts to bring the forum down will result in a permanent suspension.
Rule 3: Content
Posts are strictly limited to a maximum of 2 images per post (PNG, JPG, WEBP, or GIF up to 2MB each). Videos, executable files, or inappropriate/NSFW image uploads are strictly not allowed.
Rule 4: Relevant content
Keep discussions related to game tools, modding, OneShot, or general topics. Advertisements, scams, or malicious payload distribution will be removed.
Rule 5: Moderator actions & appeals
Forum moderators have the right to delete posts, mute users, kick users, or issue permanent account suspensions, at any time, for anything.

If you believe your account was suspended by mistake, you may submit an appeal by emailing appeals.kipforum@gmail.com with your username, moderation reason, and explanation.

Privacy policy & Data handling

1. Information we collect
When you interact within the forum, the following data is processed:
  • Usernames: The display name you choose when creating a post or comment (saved in localStorage for your convenience).
  • Content: Post titles, message text, and up to 2 uploaded images (PNG, JPG, WEBP, or GIF).
  • Anonymous IP hashes: For security and anti-abuse purposes, IP addresses from users are automatically salted and hashed with SHA-256 encryption. Raw IP addresses are never logged or stored anywhere. Moderators, and the site owner cannot view your location or your IP address.
2. How your data is used
The IP hashes are used exclusively for rate limiting, bot prevention, and moderator actions (such as mutes, kicks, or bans). We do not sell, track, or share user data with third-party companies.
3. Cookies & localStorage
This website does not use cookies. We use your localStorage to remember your chosen display name and selected theme preference across visits. You can clear this data at any time through your respective browser settings.
4. Data erasure & Removal requests
If you wish to request the deletion of a specific post or comment, please send an email to appeals.kipforum@gmail.com providing details of the post and your username, and details why it should be removed. We will review your request and remove the content if necessary.
